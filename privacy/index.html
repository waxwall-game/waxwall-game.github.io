Waxwall – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Waxwall is a tower defense game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: Waxwall does not collect any personal information.

Information stored on your device

Waxwall stores your game progress (which levels you have won and how many stars you earned) on your device. This data never leaves your device, except as part of your own Android device backup if you have backups turned on. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What Waxwall does not do

Waxwall does not connect to the internet, does not show ads, has no in-app purchases, does not add analytics or tracking, does not ask for your name or email, and does not request any device permissions — no contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. We do not collect, sell or share any information with third parties.

Children

Waxwall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that uses data (for example Google Play Games achievements), this policy will be updated on this page with a new effective date before that version is released.
