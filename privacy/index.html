Waxwall – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Waxwall is a tower defense game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: Waxwall itself does not collect personal information. If you use Google Play Games, your achievements are shared with Google so it can show them to you.

Information stored on your device

Waxwall stores your game progress on your device: which levels you have won and how many stars you earned, which enemies you have met, whether you have seen the how-to-play guide, and your achievement progress (counts such as enemies defeated or walls repaired), including which achievements have already been sent to Google Play Games. This data never leaves your device, except as part of your own Android device backup if you have backups turned on. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Waxwall sends your achievement unlocks and progress to Google Play Games Services so Google Play Games can show your achievements. The Google Play Games software in the app also sends Google diagnostic and usage data to keep the service stable. Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your player name and avatar) and your achievements under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play Waxwall without signing in; your progress then stays on your device, and achievements you earned are sent if you sign in later. You can delete your Play Games data from your Play Games profile.

What Waxwall does not do

Waxwall does not show ads, has no in-app purchases, does not add its own analytics or tracking, does not ask for your name or email, and does not request any device permissions — no contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. We do not sell or share any information with third parties.

Children

Waxwall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If a future version uses data in a new way, this policy will be updated on this page with a new effective date before that version is released.
